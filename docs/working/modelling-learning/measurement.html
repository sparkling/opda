Reading the figure

  1. Both fictional accounts use the same dimensions and unchanged geometry for Flat 1.
  2. Account A includes 68 square metres and excludes a separately identified enclosed area of 4 square metres.
  3. Account B includes both: 68 + 4 = 72 square metres. Under those premises the numbers need not conflict.
  4. The drawing is not to scale and invents no professional measurement standard. In the changed-evidence exercise, the reconciliation must be justified again.
