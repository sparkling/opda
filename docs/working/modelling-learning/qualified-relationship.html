Keep the setting with the relationship

Alex participates as buyer in a stated transaction and period concerning Flat 1. A bare person-to-dwelling link loses that setting.

Reading the figure

  1. Alex is a person. Buyer is a role Alex has in transaction T, not Alex's permanent identity.
  2. The participation needs the arrangement, the property subject and its stated period.
  3. A person-to-dwelling link without those qualifications cannot tell the reader which transaction or period is intended.
