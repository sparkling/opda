A role is not a permission

Domain participation, a scoped permission and enforcement are different things. Describing any of them in a model does not automatically grant access.

Dark specimen

Reading the figure

  1. Participation describes who acts in a role within an arrangement and period.
  2. Permission concerns an actor, action, information and scope, including relevant purpose and conditions. A domain role alone does not grant it.
  3. Enforcement checks and controls access in an implementation. Describing permissions is not itself enforcement.
  4. These distinctions do not establish legal permission, consent or lawful basis; consult the applicable policy and legal authorities.
