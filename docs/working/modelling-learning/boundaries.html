Four useful ideas, not four interchangeable boxes

A profession supplies expertise, a working group organises participation, a system handles records and a semantic context owns a meaning within its stated boundary.

Reading the figure

  1. A profession is a field of expertise and practice; members may contribute to several groups.
  2. A working group organises people. Its membership does not by itself define a semantic context.
  3. A system handles records. One system can handle meanings from several contexts.
  4. Established semantic contexts locate meaning and responsibility. No one-to-one mapping between these four ideas is asserted.
