Reading the figure

  1. Report v1 was issued on 14 August 2026 and describes the inspection of Flat 1 on 12 August.
  2. Report v2 was issued on 20 August 2026 and revises v1. It describes the same inspection, not a second visit.
  3. The issue date belongs to each report version. The inspection date belongs to the inspection. A form, tree or graph can preserve these same distinctions.
