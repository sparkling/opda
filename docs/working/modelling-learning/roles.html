Reading the figure

  1. In fictional arrangement A, Sam and Jo are borrowers and North Bank is lender, from 1 September to 31 December.
  2. In fictional arrangement B, Priya is borrower, Sam is guarantor and North Bank is lender, from 15 September to 31 December.
  3. The same Sam participates in both. Role, arrangement and period remain distinct; participation does not grant permission to data.
