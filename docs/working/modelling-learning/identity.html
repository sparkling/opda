Containment does not erase separate identities

Harbour Court contains two dwellings. Its address and a title record remain different subjects; neither is equated with the building.

Reading the figure

  1. Harbour Court is the building. Flat 1 and Flat 2 are its contained dwellings, not alternative names for the building.
  2. The address describes location. Sharing it is not enough to establish that two records concern the same subject.
  3. The title record is separately identified and has its own stated extent. No same-identity assertion is made between it and the physical building.
