A relation, a relator and a statement are different things

Flat 1 partOf Building B is a direct relation; an agreement may be a material relator, while an assertion record is about that partOf claim.

Light

Dark

  1. A direct property can express a domain relation between two instances: for example, Flat 1 partOf Building B. Its domain and range carry formal semantics.
  2. A material relator is an independently justified dependent domain entity: a candidate Agreement has its own attributes and mediates at least two bearers. The scoped method requires both.
  3. An assertion record is about the claim “Flat 1 partOf Building B” and records information such as its author and assertion time. This statement description is not thereby a material relator.
