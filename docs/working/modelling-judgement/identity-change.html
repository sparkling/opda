Keep the thing distinct from its changing descriptions

A change of state, a new description and a possible end of identity are different events. Continuity needs a stated criterion, not a reused identifier.

Dark

  1. A building may remain Building B while its occupancy or condition changes, provided the adopted identity criterion supports that continuity.
  2. A later record can revise an earlier description without changing the building or the historical event it describes.
  3. Subdivision, amalgamation or demolition require an explicit identity review. This illustration does not settle whether a new entity has begun.
  4. The diagram shows logical sequence, not a measured time scale.
