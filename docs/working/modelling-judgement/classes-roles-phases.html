Kinds, roles and phases

Kind, role and phase classify the same individual; roleOf and phaseOf are governed local class-level annotations, not subclass edges or instance participations.

Light

Dark

  1. Person is the illustrative identity-supplying kind. The actual criterion still requires domain justification.
  2. Buyer is an illustrative role grounded in a relevant external relation; someone may cease being a buyer and remain the same person.
  3. An age-defined adult classification is an illustrative phase grounded in an intrinsic condition under an explicit classification criterion.
  4. The local class-level annotations Buyer roleOf Person and Adult phaseOf Person identify bearer types; they are not rdfs:subClassOf edges or instance-level participation assertions. The individual retains the bearer’s identity under its justified criterion, rather than acquiring a new identity from role or phase membership.
