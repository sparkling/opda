# LinkedIn recruitment copy for five bounded-context working groups

## Post

Property data standards should be shaped by the people who use the data — not only by the
people who write the schemas.

Following the start of our Finance and Banking work, the Open Property Data Association is
inviting expressions of interest for five further working groups:

- Conveyancing
- Estate Agency
- Surveying and Valuation
- Property Data Services
- Property Technology

Together, these groups will help shape the **Smart Property Data Trust Framework**: a governed
family of connected domain models that preserves the meaning needed within each part of the
property journey while making exchange between them clearer and more reliable.

We want to hear from practitioners, subject-matter experts, professional bodies, data and
product specialists, and technology providers.

You can contribute by:

- sharing authorised forms, standards, schemas, definitions, guidance and examples your
  organisation already uses;
- explaining domain language, rules and real-world exceptions;
- reviewing diagrams, definitions and model candidates;
- testing familiar outputs such as schemas and forms; and
- challenging drafts when the model does not reflect practice.

You are not being asked to understand ontologies, adopt AI, or have technical knowledge. OPDA
turns evidence into reviewable model candidates; domain experts judge whether the meaning is
right.

Interested? Register and select the area or areas where you can contribute:

https://opda.org.uk/join

You bring the knowledge. OPDA makes it reviewable, reusable and ready to connect.
