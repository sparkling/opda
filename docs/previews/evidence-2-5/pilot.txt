Use case: infographic-diagram. Purpose: help property-data stakeholders understand how evidence becomes a reviewed draft through publication, human review and revision.
Create ONE complete wide landscape illustration, target 1280 x 512 pixels, aspect ratio 2.5:1. Render all artwork, labels and arrows yourself in the specified medium.
LAYOUT: six distinct stations with clear boundaries and generous connecting space. Centres as percentages of canvas: Source material (10,52), Prepare candidate (33,52), Working-group review (67,52), Draft standard (90,52), Collect feedback (50,18), Publish candidate (50,83). Each station at most 16% of canvas width and 25% of height. Keep at least 3% outer margin. The upper and lower stations are part of the loop, not extra rows in a linear sequence.
SUBJECTS: source = authorised evidence folios; prepare = practitioner revising a candidate sheet; publish = an open document portfolio displayed for others; review = two adult practitioners examining the same sheet; feedback = response slips gathered into a distinct tray; draft = a bound working manuscript without approval seals.
EXACT LABELS, each once, in crisp large dark sans-serif lettering on clean paper plaques integrated into the artwork: "Source material", "Prepare candidate", "Publish candidate", "Working-group review", "Collect feedback", "Draft standard". Two-line labels are allowed. No body copy, numbers, title, logo or other lettering except the three arrow labels below.
EXACTLY SIX DIRECTED ARROWS, one arrowhead at each destination:
1. Source material RIGHT edge to Prepare candidate LEFT edge, pointing RIGHT. Label "Initial intake".
2. Prepare candidate BOTTOM edge to Publish candidate LEFT edge, travelling down then right, arrowhead pointing into Publish candidate.
3. Publish candidate RIGHT edge to Working-group review BOTTOM edge, travelling right then up, arrowhead pointing UP into review.
4. Working-group review TOP edge to Collect feedback RIGHT edge, travelling up then left, arrowhead pointing LEFT into feedback. Label "Changes requested".
5. Collect feedback LEFT edge to Prepare candidate TOP edge, travelling left then down, arrowhead pointing DOWN into preparation.
6. Working-group review RIGHT edge to Draft standard LEFT edge, pointing RIGHT. Label "Consensus reached".
Arrows 1-5 violet; arrow 6 gold with dark outline for contrast. Use consistent shaft thickness and arrowhead size. Keep each connector visually continuous and separated from text, objects and other arrows. Leave visible space at every arrowhead so its destination is unambiguous. No other lines resembling connections. Source has no incoming arrow. Draft has no outgoing arrow. Review has two outgoing arrows; preparation has two incoming arrows. The draft is reviewed, not ratified or adopted.
