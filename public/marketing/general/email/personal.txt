The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting people with experience across the property journey to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. The working groups are developing shared definitions for property information so that people can understand and exchange it more clearly.

Help explain what information means in your work, which exceptions matter and what other people need to understand when you share it.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.
