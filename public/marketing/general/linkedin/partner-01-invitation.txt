The invitation

What would make property information more useful in your work?

We are sharing an opportunity from the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) for people with experience across the property journey to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Help explain what information means in your work, which exceptions matter and what other people need to understand when you share it.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join
