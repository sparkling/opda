The invitation

Your experience can help shape better property information.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we are inviting people with experience across the property journey to contribute to the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Help explain what information means in your work, which exceptions matter and what other people need to understand when you share it.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

A question from real work

A property transaction brings together information from many organisations. Repeated questions and differences in meaning make that information harder to use.

An address can help locate a property, but it does not by itself explain whether a report describes a building, a dwelling or a title record.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), our working groups need professional experience to help make these distinctions clear. Help explain what information means in your work, which exceptions matter and what other people need to understand when you share it.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

What you can contribute

Your professional experience belongs in the discussion.

You do not need to be a data modeller to help improve property information.

Contributors can explain the language and rules used in their work, bring authorised examples, review draft definitions and challenge assumptions.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we want the definitions to reflect real work, including difficult cases.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.

Find your working group: https://opda.org.uk/join
