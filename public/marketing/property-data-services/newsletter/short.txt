Bring your experience to OPDA’s working groups

The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting property data providers and people responsible for sourcing or assuring information to help develop shared definitions for property information through the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. No data-modelling expertise is required. Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access. Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=property-data-services
