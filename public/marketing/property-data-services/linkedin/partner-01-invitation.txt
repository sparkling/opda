The invitation

What would make property information more useful in your work?

We are sharing an opportunity from the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) for property data providers and people responsible for sourcing or assuring information to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Bring examples that make provenance, quality and limitations easier for other organisations to understand.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=property-data-services
