The invitation

Your experience can help shape better property information.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we are inviting property data providers and people responsible for sourcing or assuring information to contribute to the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Bring examples that make provenance, quality and limitations easier for other organisations to understand.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=property-data-services

A question from real work

Property records come from sources with different coverage and update cycles. People need to understand what a record describes, where it came from and how it can be used.

Matching two records to an address does not automatically establish that they describe the same thing.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), our working groups need professional experience to help make these distinctions clear. Bring examples that make provenance, quality and limitations easier for other organisations to understand.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=property-data-services

What you can contribute

Your professional experience belongs in the discussion.

You do not need to be a data modeller to help improve property information.

Contributors can explain the language and rules used in their work, bring authorised examples, review draft definitions and challenge assumptions.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we want the definitions to reflect real work, including difficult cases.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.

Find your working group: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=property-data-services
