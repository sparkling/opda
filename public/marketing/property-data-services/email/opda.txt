Help bring your members’ experience into the work

I am writing on behalf of the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) about the development of the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

We would welcome contributions from property data providers and people responsible for sourcing or assuring information. Property records come from sources with different coverage and update cycles. People need to understand what a record describes, where it came from and how it can be used.

Bring examples that make provenance, quality and limitations easier for other organisations to understand.

Could you share the invitation with your members through an email, newsletter or LinkedIn post? A campaign pack with a member email, social posts and a short presentation is available at https://opda.org.uk/marketing/packs/property-data-services. Please adapt the material freely to suit your organisation and audience.

You retain your relationship with your members. Please distribute through your own channels; we do not need your mailing list.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.
