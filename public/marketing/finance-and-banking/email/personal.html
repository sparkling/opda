Finance and Banking

A contribution you might be interested in

I thought this opportunity might be relevant to your work.

The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting lenders, mortgage advisers and people working with finance and banking information to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. The working groups are developing shared definitions for property information so that people can understand and exchange it more clearly.

Bring examples of the evidence lenders and advisers need, where information is repeated or unclear, and what helps people make informed decisions.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.
