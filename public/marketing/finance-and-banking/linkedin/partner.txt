What would make property information more useful in your work?

We are sharing an opportunity from the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) for lenders, mortgage advisers and people working with finance and banking information to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Bring examples of the evidence lenders and advisers need, where information is repeated or unclear, and what helps people make informed decisions.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=finance-and-banking
