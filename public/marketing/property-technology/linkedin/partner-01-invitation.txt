The invitation

What would make property information more useful in your work?

We are sharing an opportunity from the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) for property technology teams and people designing or integrating property services to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Explain where clearer definitions would help services work together and which requirements need to reflect existing products and users.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=property-technology
