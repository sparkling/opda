Property Technology

Help bring your members’ experience into the work

I am writing on behalf of the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) about the development of the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

We would welcome contributions from property technology teams and people designing or integrating property services. Products exchange information across systems that often use different labels and assumptions. An apparently simple field can conceal a difference in meaning.

Explain where clearer definitions would help services work together and which requirements need to reflect existing products and users.

Could you share the invitation with your members through an email, newsletter or LinkedIn post? A campaign pack with a member email, social posts and a short presentation is available at https://opda.org.uk/marketing/packs/property-technology. Please adapt the material freely to suit your organisation and audience.

You retain your relationship with your members. Please distribute through your own channels; we do not need your mailing list.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.
