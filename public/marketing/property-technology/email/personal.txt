The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting property technology teams and people designing or integrating property services to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. The working groups are developing shared definitions for property information so that people can understand and exchange it more clearly.

Explain where clearer definitions would help services work together and which requirements need to reflect existing products and users.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.
