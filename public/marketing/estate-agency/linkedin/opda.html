The invitation

Your experience can help shape better property information.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we are inviting estate agents and people involved in marketing and progressing property sales to contribute to the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Share what people need to know at each stage and where clearer information could reduce repeated questions or misunderstandings.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

A question from real work

Property information develops from instruction through marketing and sales progression. Buyers, sellers and other professionals need to understand what has changed and what remains uncertain.

A listing description and a later professional finding may describe different aspects of the same home.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), our working groups need professional experience to help make these distinctions clear. Share what people need to know at each stage and where clearer information could reduce repeated questions or misunderstandings.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

What you can contribute

Your professional experience belongs in the discussion.

You do not need to be a data modeller to help improve property information.

Contributors can explain the language and rules used in their work, bring authorised examples, review draft definitions and challenge assumptions.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we want the definitions to reflect real work, including difficult cases.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.

Find your working group: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=estate-agency
