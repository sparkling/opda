The invitation

What would make property information more useful in your work?

We are sharing an opportunity from the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) for estate agents and people involved in marketing and progressing property sales to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Share what people need to know at each stage and where clearer information could reduce repeated questions or misunderstandings.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=estate-agency
