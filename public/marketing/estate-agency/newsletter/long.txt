Bring your experience to OPDA’s working groups

The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting estate agents and people involved in marketing and progressing property sales to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. The working groups are developing shared definitions for property information so that people can understand and exchange it more clearly.

Share what people need to know at each stage and where clearer information could reduce repeated questions or misunderstandings.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.

Explore the working groups: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=estate-agency
