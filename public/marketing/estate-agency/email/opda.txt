I am writing on behalf of the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) about the development of the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

We would welcome contributions from estate agents and people involved in marketing and progressing property sales. Property information develops from instruction through marketing and sales progression. Buyers, sellers and other professionals need to understand what has changed and what remains uncertain.

Share what people need to know at each stage and where clearer information could reduce repeated questions or misunderstandings.

Could you share the invitation with your members through an email, newsletter or LinkedIn post? A campaign pack with a member email, social posts and a short presentation is available at https://opda.org.uk/marketing/packs/estate-agency. Please adapt the material freely to suit your organisation and audience.

You retain your relationship with your members. Please distribute through your own channels; we do not need your mailing list.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.
