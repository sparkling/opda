Help shape property information for surveying and valuation

Your experience can help shape the work

We are sharing an opportunity for our members to contribute their experience to the future of property information.

The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting surveyors, valuers and people who use their findings to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. The working groups are developing shared definitions for property information so that people can understand and exchange it more clearly.

An inspection or valuation produces findings that others rely on. Observations, professional judgements and limitations need to remain distinguishable when those findings are shared.

Help explain how measurements and findings should carry their basis, qualifications and uncertainty beyond the original report.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.
