Your experience can help shape better property information.

At the Open Property Data Association (OPDA), we are inviting surveyors, valuers and people who use their findings to contribute to the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Help explain how measurements and findings should carry their basis, qualifications and uncertainty beyond the original report.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=surveying-and-valuation
