A question from real work

A conveyance draws on title records, searches and enquiries from different sources. Important qualifications can become difficult to follow as that information moves between people.

A statement in an enquiry and evidence that supports it play different parts in understanding a property.

The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting professional experience to help make these distinctions clear. Explain the distinctions that matter in a real conveyance and help make definitions useful for exceptions as well as straightforward transactions.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.
