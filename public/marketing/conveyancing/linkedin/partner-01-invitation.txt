The invitation

What would make property information more useful in your work?

We are sharing an opportunity from the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) for conveyancers and people working with property transfer information to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

Explain the distinctions that matter in a real conveyance and help make definitions useful for exceptions as well as straightforward transactions.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registration expresses interest, subject to OPDA review, not automatic membership or access.

Explore the working groups and register your interest: https://opda.org.uk/join?context=conveyancing
