Conveyancing

Your experience can help shape the work

We are sharing an opportunity for our members to contribute their experience to the future of property information.

The Open Property Data Association (OPDA) is inviting conveyancers and people working with property transfer information to help develop the Smart Property Data Trust Framework. The working groups are developing shared definitions for property information so that people can understand and exchange it more clearly.

A conveyance draws on title records, searches and enquiries from different sources. Important qualifications can become difficult to follow as that information moves between people.

Explain the distinctions that matter in a real conveyance and help make definitions useful for exceptions as well as straightforward transactions.

You do not need data-modelling expertise. Your professional experience and judgement can help shape the work.

Registering is an expression of interest. OPDA reviews each submission and confirms the next steps. It does not automatically give membership or access.
