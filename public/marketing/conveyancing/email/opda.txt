Help bring your members’ experience into the work

I am writing on behalf of the Open Property Data Association (OPDA) about the development of the Smart Property Data Trust Framework.

We would welcome contributions from conveyancers and people working with property transfer information. A conveyance draws on title records, searches and enquiries from different sources. Important qualifications can become difficult to follow as that information moves between people.

Explain the distinctions that matter in a real conveyance and help make definitions useful for exceptions as well as straightforward transactions.

Could you share the invitation with your members through an email, newsletter or LinkedIn post? A campaign pack with a member email, social posts and a short presentation is available at https://opda.org.uk/marketing/packs/conveyancing. You retain your relationship with your members. Please distribute through your own channels; we do not need your mailing list.
